AgentQuest Reputation Score (v1) — our own score, not a Binance score. Max 1,000.

How it’s calculated: Agent ID 100 · Badges 50 each (max 200) · Quest Contributions 30 per Sparks (max 150) + 30 per campaign completion (max 150) · Consistency 3 per streak day (max 90) + 2 per check-in (max 60) · Partner Endorsement and Community 0 — coming soon. Levels: Bronze 0–249 · Silver 250–499 · Gold 500–749 · Platinum 750–999 · Diamond 1,000.

The campaign half of Quest Contributions (150 of 300 pts) is empty for everyone right now — campaigns launch Dec 2026. That’s where most future points come from.
