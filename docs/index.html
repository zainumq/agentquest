    How it works

    1. Connect your wallet (MetaMask or Binance Wallet) on BNB Smart Chain.
    2. Check — the page reads the contract's eligibility views for your wallet. No transaction is sent.
    3. Claim — each eligible badge is one transaction you confirm in your wallet. Gas only, value 0.
    • Contract: not deployed yet View on BscScan
    • Agent ID registry: 0x60b9…022f
    • Standard: ERC-721, soulbound (ERC-5192) — badges cannot be transferred or sold.
    • Metadata: fully on-chain SVG, no IPFS or server.
    • Admin: none. No owner, no pause, no upgrade, no fees, no withdraw function.
