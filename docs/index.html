Earn soulbound badges by proving, purely on-chain, that you hold NFTs from Agent ID ecosystem collections. No server, no voucher, no sign-up. Claims cost gas only — the contract accepts no payment at all.

Badges are non-transferable achievement records (ERC-5192). They have no price and cannot be sold.

Daily Check-in

Connect your wallet to check your streak.

Check in once a day to build a streak. Each check-in is one transaction, gas only (about Rp100), value 0 — no approval, no fee. Streaks of 3, 7, 14 and 30 days auto-mint soulbound milestone badges, and your total check-ins count toward a share of a 3,000,000 KONG pool at $KONG graduation.
