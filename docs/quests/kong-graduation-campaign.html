25 winners × $20 + 20,000 KONG. Every wallet that completes the quest has the same chance — points are already rewarded on the leaderboard, so the raffle is not weighted. The 10 leaderboard winners are not entered in the raffle, so prizes spread across 35 different wallets.

How to join

Complete all four requirements inside the campaign window (3–17 Dec 2026):

  1. Hold at least 1 AgentQuest badge. Verified on-chain (live balanceOf). No badge yet? Claim one free — gas only.
  2. Check in on at least 3 different days during the window. Verified on-chain via the Streak contract — check in on the home page, gas only.
  3. One public post about the $KONG sale (on X or in The Colony). Posts are reviewed manually by the AgentQuest team — the curator's decision is final for this campaign.
  4. Follow @fer_rodrigues06 (AgentQuest) on X. Checked manually against the follower list when the window closes.

There is no join button: this campaign is tracked manually in its first edition. Do the four steps above, and your wallet is in.

  • Prizes are paid only if $KONG graduation is verified. Graduation means all 10,000 sale shares are sold. If the sale does not graduate, quest participants receive nothing.
