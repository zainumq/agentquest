How to join

Complete both requirements inside the campaign window (3–17 Dec 2026):

  1. Hold at least 1 AgentQuest badge. Verified on-chain (live balanceOf). No badge yet? Claim one free — gas only.
  2. Check in on SYMPHONY Soundcheck on 7 consecutive days during the window. Check in on the SYMPHONY site — gas only. Anyone starting by 11 Dec can complete 7 days by 17 Dec.

There is no join button: this campaign is tracked manually in its first edition. Do the two steps above, and your wallet is in.

Soundcheck counts consecutive daily check-ins. A 7-day streak also mints the REHEARSAL badge on Soundcheck automatically.

The road does not end at day 7 — the Opus Magnum

A participant who starts on 3 Dec and never misses reaches day 14 on 16 Dec — the Opus Magnum, SYMPHONY's full-set crown, which shares the 30M OPUS Soundcheck pool if the sale graduates. So the road does not end at the 7-day reward; it runs straight into SYMPHONY's endgame, and into the sale's first days.

The Magnum and the 30M OPUS Soundcheck pool are SYMPHONY's own rewards, separate from this campaign's reputation reward. They are paid only if the SYMPHONY sale graduates.

Everything this campaign needs is on-chain. The Soundcheck contract (0xf29BB5C9266e4bD829be23cC9878e241fb85DD71) exposes streakOf and totalCheckInsOf, and every check-in emits a CheckedIn event — so a streak can be verified from public chain data alone, with nothing needed from SYMPHONY's side.
